# Vendly.lk Product Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Purpose

Vendly.lk is a multi-seller commerce and order-management platform for small and medium-sized businesses. Sellers can publish products, share short product and store links, answer customer questions through chat, receive multi-product orders, manage inventory, coordinate couriers, and analyse business performance.

This document records approved product requirements. The features are planned, but they should be developed in phases rather than all at once.

## Public link formats

- Product link: `https://vendly.lk/p/P8x43K`
- Seller mini-store link: `https://vendly.lk/s/V7k92M`
- Customer referral link: `https://vendly.lk/r/K8m42P`

Short codes must be unique, non-sequential, generated by the backend, and resolved only after confirming that the related seller and resource are active.

## Product and inventory features

### Product variations

A product may have variations based on:

- Colour
- Size
- Storage capacity
- Material
- Model

Each sellable variation must have its own SKU, barcode, price, weight, status, and inventory level.

### Product bundles

Sellers can combine several products into a discounted bundle. For example:

- Smart Watch + Earbuds
- Normal price: LKR 9,000
- Bundle price: LKR 8,200

A bundle does not maintain independent physical stock. When a bundle is sold, stock must be reserved or deducted from every component product or variation in one transaction.

### Related-product suggestions

Vendly can suggest related products when a customer views or orders a product. The first version should use category relationships and sales history. AI-based recommendations can be considered later.

### Back-in-stock notifications

Customers can subscribe to an unavailable product or variation. When stock becomes available, Vendly can notify the customer through supported channels such as WhatsApp, email, web, or mobile notifications.

Subscriptions must be deduplicated and allow customers to unsubscribe.

### Wishlist and saved cart

- Logged-in customers can maintain a persistent wishlist and cart.
- Guest customers can use a temporary browser or chatbot cart.
- Temporary carts must expire after a configured period.
- Product prices and availability must be validated again during checkout.

### Batch and expiry tracking

Applicable products may record:

- Batch number
- Manufacture date
- Expiry date
- Quantity
- Supplier
- Storage location

Where appropriate, inventory should follow the first-expiry-first-out method.

### Multiple shops and warehouses

A business can have several shops, branches, or warehouses. Inventory levels must be maintained per product variation and location.

Stock transfers must create two linked inventory transactions:

1. Stock-out from the origin location.
2. Stock-in to the destination location.

## Orders, payments, and documents

### Deposits and partial payments

Orders can support:

- Full payment
- Deposit payment
- Multiple partial payments
- Remaining cash-on-delivery balance
- Refunds

Payment records must be stored separately from the order. The backend must calculate the paid amount, refunded amount, remaining balance, and COD amount.

### Business documents

Vendly can generate downloadable and printable:

- Quotations
- Invoices
- Payment receipts
- Packing slips
- Waybills
- Return receipts

Documents should contain the seller's logo, business information, customer information, order number, item details, totals, and relevant delivery information.

### Returns and exchanges

The return workflow can include:

1. Return requested
2. Return approved
3. Item collected
4. Item inspected
5. Refunded, replaced, or rejected

Vendly must record the reason, item condition, photographs, responsible courier, staff actions, payment effects, and inventory effects.

## Customer management

### Customer tags and groups

Supported classifications may include:

- New customer
- Repeat customer
- VIP customer
- Wholesale customer
- High return rate
- Payment pending

Businesses may also create custom tags.

### Private customer notes

Authorised staff can save internal notes such as delivery preferences or instructions. Private notes must never be returned through public customer, catalogue, tracking, or chatbot endpoints.

### Referral programme

Referral links must track:

- Referring customer
- Referred customer
- Eligible completed order
- Reward earned
- Reward status

Rewards should be issued only after the qualifying order reaches the required final status.

## Marketing and storefront features

### Coupon campaigns

Supported coupon types:

- Percentage discount
- Fixed discount
- Free delivery
- Product-specific discount

Coupon rules may include:

- Minimum order value
- Total usage limit
- Per-customer usage limit
- Start date
- Expiry date
- Eligible products or categories

The backend must validate every coupon and control whether multiple discounts can be combined.

### Promotion calendar

Sellers can schedule:

- Promotion start and end dates
- Promotional product prices
- Social-media content
- Customer notifications

The system should retain the normal price and restore it correctly after a scheduled promotion.

### Shareable mini-store

Every seller can have a public mini-store using a link such as `https://vendly.lk/s/V7k92M`. It can display active products, approved reviews, seller contact information, and a button to open the seller-specific chatbot.

## Dashboard and reporting

### Daily work centre

The dashboard should prioritise actions, including:

- Orders requiring confirmation
- Orders waiting to be packed
- Products requiring restocking
- Unread messages
- Unprinted waybills
- Courier delays
- Unreconciled COD payments
- Batches nearing expiry

### Product profitability

Profit calculations may use:

- Selling price
- Product cost snapshot
- Discounts
- Seller-paid delivery amount
- Tax
- Return and refund costs
- Payment or courier charges

Order items must save price and cost snapshots so historical reports do not change when product prices change.

### Dead-stock report

The report should identify products that:

- Have not sold during a configurable period
- Occupy inventory space
- Produce low profit margins
- May benefit from a discount or bundle

### Cash-on-delivery reconciliation

Track:

- Delivered order value
- Amount collected by the courier
- Courier charges
- Expected settlement
- Received settlement
- Missing or disputed amount
- Settlement date and reference

Courier settlement status must remain separate from delivery status.

### Staff performance

Subject to roles and permissions, Vendly may report:

- Orders processed
- Average response time
- Packing errors
- Customer ratings
- Stock adjustments

Important staff actions must be supported by audit logs. Performance information must not be publicly accessible.

## Messaging and productivity

### Voice-message support

Customers can send voice messages through supported chat channels. Vendly may transcribe them and display the transcription to the seller.

The system must retain access to the original recording because automated transcription may be incorrect. Audio access, consent, and retention rules must protect customer privacy.

### Keyboard and barcode shortcuts

Planned shortcuts include:

- `Ctrl + K`: Open global search
- `N`: Create a new order
- `B`: Scan a barcode
- `P`: Print a waybill

Shortcuts must not activate while the user is typing inside a form field.

### Global search

One search interface should find authorised records by:

- Order number
- Customer name
- Phone number
- Product name
- SKU
- Barcode
- Waybill number
- Courier tracking number

Every result must be restricted to the authenticated user's business and permissions.

## Foundational data rules

- All seller-owned data must be scoped by `businessId`.
- React must not calculate or authoritatively change totals, stock, permissions, or order numbers.
- Flask must validate ownership, permissions, totals, discounts, payments, and stock changes.
- Inventory changes must create immutable transaction records.
- Important multi-document changes must use Firestore transactions.
- Files belong in Firebase Storage; Firestore stores their metadata and authorised references.
- Sensitive secrets and provider credentials must remain in the backend or a secret manager.
- Private customer and staff information must never be exposed through public endpoints.
- AI may assist with content or recommendations, but it must not control money, inventory, permissions, fraud decisions, or final order status.

## Delivery principle

Complete and verify one end-to-end workflow before expanding the next phase:

`Share product -> customer conversation -> order -> confirmation -> stock update -> packing -> courier -> waybill -> delivery -> settlement`

## Proposed analytics roadmap

These items are future work unless explicitly listed as implemented in `README.md` or `docs/api-endpoints.md`.

### Priority 1 — financial control

- **Implemented foundation:** product gross profitability after allocated order
  discounts, recorded product cost and product-linked warranty deductions.
- Extend product gross profitability into contribution margin by allocating
  courier charges and return-handling costs per product.
- COD reconciliation: expected collection, courier deductions, settlement received, variance and overdue settlements.
- Implemented: Analytics now records separate per-order settlements and shows
  courier charges, received settlement, variance, overdue state and disputes.
- Operating-expense ledger for rent, salaries, utilities, marketing and tax so Vendly can calculate true net profit.

### Priority 2 — stock and delivery decisions

- Stock turnover, days of inventory, dead stock and projected stock-out date.
- Reorder suggestions based on recent sales velocity and supplier lead time.
- Courier performance by district: delivery fee, success rate, median delivery time, return rate and branch-issue count.

### Priority 3 — customer and growth analytics

- New versus repeat customer cohorts, lifetime value and average reorder interval.
- Channel comparison for chatbot, storefront, manual online orders and physical-shop sales.
- Catalogue-to-order funnel: product view, detail view, cart, checkout, confirmed and delivered.
- Promotion and coupon attribution based on revenue and margin, not order count alone.

### Data-quality requirement

Warn about products without cost price, category, weight or supplier and orders without courier, district or payment status. Missing source data makes financial and operational analytics misleading.
